About me

Hello! I'm Hunain Chhipa, a Full Stack Developer with 2.5+ years of professional experience building production-ready web applications. I specialize in the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) and modern frontend frameworks, delivering scalable solutions for real-world business challenges.

Currently at Vizograph Solutions, I build interactive chart editors and data visualization tools using ApexCharts and React. Previously at Wanbuffer Services, I developed enterprise applications including multilingual contract management systems and email verification platforms.

My flagship project, StyleCreation, is a comprehensive garment workflow management system used in production environments to track orders, vendors, and manufacturing stages from entry to delivery. This project showcases my ability to architect full-stack solutions with real-time analytics, AWS integration, and complex state management.
